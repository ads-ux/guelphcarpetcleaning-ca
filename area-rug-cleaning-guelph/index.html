Area Rug Cleaning in Guelph

Area rugs hold more dirt than wall-to-wall carpet. They sit in entryways, under dining tables and in front of the couch, and the sand and grit that gets ground into the base wears the fibres down from underneath. A proper clean every 12 to 24 months keeps a good rug looking right and lasting longer.

Tell us about your rug and we'll connect you with a local carpet and rug cleaner serving Guelph, Fergus, Elora, Rockwood, Puslinch and the rest of Wellington County.

Rugs we can help with

  • Wool rugs — the most common "good" rug. Wool cleans well but shrinks, bleeds dye and browns if it is cleaned too hot or left wet, so it needs a low-heat, wool-safe approach.
  • Synthetic rugs (polypropylene, nylon, polyester) — durable and colourfast. Most can be cleaned in place with hot water extraction.
  • Hand-knotted and oriental rugs — natural dyes and cotton foundations need a gentler process. Fringes are washed by hand.
  • Viscose, silk and jute — these fibres water-mark and lose their shape easily. Let us know up front so the right method is used, or so you get an honest "this one needs a specialist."

In your home or taken away?

Most synthetic and many wool rugs can be cleaned on site the same visit as your carpets. Heavily soiled rugs, pet urine that has soaked through to the backing, and delicate hand-made rugs are often better cleaned off site, where they can be fully washed and dried flat. Ask when you get your quote which one your rug needs.

Pet accidents on area rugs

Urine soaks through a rug and into the backing and the floor below it. Surface cleaning removes the stain you can see but not the odour. An enzyme treatment, or a full soak for bad cases, is what actually removes the smell. If you have pets, mention it when you ask for a quote.

What affects the price

  • Size of the rug
  • Fibre type (wool and hand-made rugs take more care than synthetics)
  • How dirty it is, and whether there are pet stains or odour
  • Whether it is cleaned in your home or taken away

The quickest way to an accurate price is to send the rug's size, a photo of the front and a photo of the label or back.

Common questions

How often should an area rug be cleaned?

Every 12 to 24 months for most homes. Every 6 to 12 months in high-traffic spots, or if you have pets or kids.

How long does a rug take to dry?

A rug cleaned in place is usually dry in 4 to 8 hours with good airflow. Rugs washed off site take longer because they are fully soaked and dried flat.

Can you clean a rug the same day as my carpets?

Usually, yes. Most synthetic and wool rugs can be done in the same visit. Mention the rugs when you book so enough time is set aside.
