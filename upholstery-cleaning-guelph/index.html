Upholstery Cleaning in Guelph

Your couch gets more use than almost anything in the house, and it soaks up body oils, food, pet hair and dust. Upholstery cleaning lifts that out of the fabric and the padding underneath, and gets rid of the dull, flat look and the smell that builds up over time.

Tell us what you need cleaned and we'll connect you with a local cleaner serving Guelph and Wellington County. Upholstery is often done in the same visit as carpets.

What can be cleaned

  • Sofas, loveseats and sectionals
  • Recliners and armchairs
  • Fabric dining chairs and bar stools
  • Ottomans and headboards
  • Mattresses

Check your cleaning code first

Look for a tag under the cushions or on the base of the frame. It tells the cleaner which method is safe for your fabric:

  • W — water-based cleaning is safe. Most microfibre and synthetic blends.
  • S — solvent only. Water can leave rings or shrink the fabric.
  • W/S — either method is fine.
  • X — vacuum only. No wet or solvent cleaning.

Send a photo of the tag with your quote request and the right method can be planned before anyone shows up.

Pet hair, odour and stains

Pet hair is removed first so it doesn't get pushed deeper into the fabric. Odour from pets or spills that have reached the cushion foam needs a targeted treatment, not just a surface clean. Most food, drink and everyday stains come out. Old set-in stains, ink and dye transfer may only lighten.

What affects the price

  • Number and size of pieces (a 3-seat sofa vs. a large sectional)
  • Fabric type and cleaning code
  • Pet hair, odour or heavy soiling
  • Whether it is booked together with carpet cleaning

Common questions

How long does a couch take to dry?

Usually 4 to 8 hours. Running a fan and keeping the room warm speeds it up.

Can leather be cleaned?

Leather needs its own cleaner and conditioner, not the method used on fabric. Mention it when you ask for a quote so you get the right answer.

How often should upholstery be cleaned?

Every 12 to 24 months for most homes, more often with pets, kids or allergies.
